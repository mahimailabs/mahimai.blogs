"Pipeline or speech-to-speech?"

Every voice platform evaluation I sit in starts with that question. Then it turns into a latency argument with three vendors' numbers, measured three ways.

So we built a blind test instead.

A mock interview, two unlabelled interviewers, one LiveKit call:
→ Interviewer one: Deepgram, gpt-4o-mini, Cartesia, VAD turn detection
→ Interviewer two: OpenAI GPT-Live, full duplex, listens while it talks
→ Random order, same instructions, a code-driven handoff mid-call
→ Every reply timed from the end of your speech to first audio

The panel stays blind until you vote for the one that felt more human. Then it reveals which was which, with a waterfall per reply: turn decided, first token, first audio for the pipeline, one bar for duplex, and a flag when duplex started talking before you finished.

The number to watch is not the stage your vendor quotes. It is e2e_latency in LiveKit's message metrics, the gap the caller actually hears.

Caveats are in the post: the voices differ, and the pipeline is untuned on purpose.

Which one do you think wins the vote?
