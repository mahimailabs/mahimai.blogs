Most speaking practice features say that whatever you said. The learner says "I sink it is Thursday" and gets a thumbs up, or a 72 with no idea which word cost them.

Your speech recognizer already knows. Deepgram returns a confidence for every word, and a word it had to guess comes back low.

How our speaking coach demo uses that:
→ line the heard words up against the sentence the learner was asked to read
→ score every word in code: clear, close, weak, heard as something else, or missed
→ hand the LLM only the one or two weakest words to coach, and forbid it from inventing others
→ have the learner re-read the line, then move the level from the first read

The detail I would copy first: no keyterm boosting on the STT. Bias the recognizer toward "think" and it will hear "think" when the learner said "sink". The error you wanted to catch disappears.
