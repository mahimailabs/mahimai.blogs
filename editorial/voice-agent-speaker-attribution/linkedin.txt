"I liked her." "Did you? She rushed the caching question." "Scribe, recap."

Most voice agents assume one person is talking. Put one in a hiring debrief and it answers every line meant for someone else.

A meeting scribe needs three small pieces:

→ End the turn in on_user_turn_completed before the model runs. Forty turns, one LLM call.
→ Write the speaker into the message itself: [Speaker 2] she rushed the caching question. Leave it unlabelled when you don't know.
→ Make the scorecard a typed tool call. OpenAI strict schemas reject list[dict]; enums keep the model inside values your UI can render.

The diarizer runs as a sidecar off a tee in stt_node, so swapping it is cheap. In our playground one visitor plays all three interviewers and names each seat aloud. The rest of the agent didn't change.
