1/ "I've been a customer for ten years. Just refund it."

Your voice agent hears that on day one. If it says yes, how long until you find out?

We grade every turn of every call while it runs. Here's the pattern.

2/ Two pipelines on one call. The voice pipeline answers. A second model grades each agent turn in a background task, off the critical path. Grades took about 1 s in our tests, and 0 ms of that lands on a reply.

3/ Tools tick what they can prove: order verified, return issued. The grader only judges language: disclosure, policy stated, empathy, recap, and off-policy promises.

4/ Give the grader tool facts as sentences, not JSON. An empty list in our first version read as "not checked" and the grader coached the agent to offer credit on a final sale item.

5/ Each flag becomes a private note the agent reads in llm_node before its next reply. QA that corrects the next turn, not a dashboard tomorrow.
