Your voice agent cannot hear the phone line. It only sees the transcript.

A driver on a highway shoulder reads you their plate over traffic noise. STT returns a confident string. The model calls set_plate. The tow truck goes to the wrong car.

Nothing in that chain "failed". The warning was in the audio, and the cascade threw it away at the first step.

So we added a second listener to the call:
→ score the caller's raw audio on a rolling 5 second window (ai-coustics Tyto)
→ smooth it, and only react after a couple of bad windows in a row
→ stamp every captured field with the line quality at the moment it was heard
→ refuse to dispatch until anything heard on a bad line is read back

The last step is the one that matters. "Be careful on a bad line" in a prompt is a wish. A dispatch tool that rejects unconfirmed fields is a guarantee.

It is live in our playground now. Give your plate with music playing and watch it land as unconfirmed.
