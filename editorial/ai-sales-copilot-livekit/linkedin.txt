"To be upfront, we're already talking to your competitor."

The battle card for that line exists. It is three clicks away in a Notion page. The buyer is waiting.

We built a sales call you can take yourself to show the fix. You play the rep, an AI buyer pushes back, and a second agent on the call never says a word:
→ it hears both sides of the conversation
→ embeds what the buyer just said and matches it against a 12-card playbook
→ puts the card on your screen in about 200 ms
→ then writes one sentence you can say, about a second later

The takeaway: never make the card wait for the model. Retrieval goes on screen the moment it returns. Generation fills in after. Both are timed from when the buyer stopped talking.

The floor matters more than the model. On-topic buyer lines scored 0.48 to 0.85 against their card. Small talk stayed under 0.33. A copilot that fires on "sure, go ahead" gets ignored by week two.
