"Thank you for holding. Your call is important to us."

Somewhere in a clinic's back office, a person is listening to that on speakerphone, waiting to copy a copay off an insurance rep.

We built a voice agent that takes that call:
→ it hears each phone menu and sends real DTMF tones, with a known route if the model is slow
→ it stays silent on hold and classifies every line as recording or person
→ the moment a person speaks, it takes over on the same turn
→ every benefit the rep says goes through a tool that normalises, range-checks and keeps the quote it came from

The one I would copy first: give the hold detector a veto the model cannot override. A transcript that repeats word for word is a recording. Hold messages loop. People do not.

In our playground you play the payer rep. Say "please continue to hold" and it keeps waiting. Pick up, read the benefits out of order, change a number, and watch the record follow.
