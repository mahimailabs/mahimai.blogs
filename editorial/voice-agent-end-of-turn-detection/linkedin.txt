"Yeah, it's 42... uh... Maple Street."

Two pauses, each over half a second. A voice agent on a silence timer hears "Yeah, it's 42", decides you are done, and starts talking over you. On the one answer it cannot get wrong.

We built an emergency furnace line to show the fix, and to measure it:
→ LiveKit's audio turn detector decides when the caller is done
→ a plain silence timer runs in shadow on the same audio and only watches
→ every pause where the timer would have cut you off gets a marker, the half sentence it would have sent, and the detector's probability at that moment

The detector does not make clean answers faster. It makes unfinished ones patient. That is the trade callers notice.

One number before you ship it: the local v1-mini model took our worker from 217 MB to 477 MB resident. On a 500 MB service, that decided it. The hosted demo uses the full model on LiveKit Inference with the local fallback off, about 5 MB per call.
