400 people on the same cancelled flight call at once. Which part of your voice agent breaks first?

Usually not the model. It is silence.

Two kinds:
→ a tool that blocks the turn while it waits 8 seconds on the booking system
→ a provider that starts failing under the load, with nothing behind it

What works:
→ make slow tools async. In LiveKit, ctx.update() releases the turn and the search keeps running, so "can I keep my window seat?" gets answered mid-search
→ wrap the LLM and TTS in a FallbackAdapter, lower the attempt timeout to what a caller will sit through, and turn retries on the primary off
→ log every availability change, and decide on sticky fallback on purpose

My take: a fallback at the same provider is not a fallback. Put the backup in a different failure domain.

We built a flight rebooking demo for our playground. Ask to rebook, then say "knock out the voice" and hear the backup take over.
