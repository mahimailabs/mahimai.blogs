Your avatar agent stopped talking. Its face didn't.

Put a video avatar on a LiveKit voice agent and the avatar is not a renderer. It is a third participant in the room. Your agent streams audio to it, and it publishes the voice and the face to the caller.

That moves three things you used to get for free:
→ interruptions now have to stop someone else's buffer (lk.clear_buffer)
→ the transcript has to be cut where the face stopped, not where your agent stopped sending (lk.playback_finished carries the position)
→ latency gains a stage after TTS that none of your STT, LLM, TTS dashboards show

The test I run first on any avatar agent: interrupt it mid-sentence, then ask "what did you just say?" If it repeats words the face never spoke, playback position is not reaching your chat history.

We built a hotel concierge with an Anam avatar into our playground. Ask where to eat, cut it off, book a table, and watch join time, lip start and cut-in time live beside the face.
