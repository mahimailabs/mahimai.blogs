A customer wants $649 back. The agent can give $150. What happens next decides whether they call again.

Most voice agents do one of three things: say no, say yes and blow through policy, or "let me transfer you" and make the caller tell the whole story twice.

A good returns clerk does something else. One sentence to the manager, a nod, and back on the line in twenty seconds.

What we built:
→ the agent sends the manager a whisper brief over LiveKit RPC, built from what the tools verified, with a recommendation
→ the caller is never left in silence: scripted check-ins that wait if the caller is talking
→ the manager approves, counters with store credit, declines, or takes the call
→ no answer in 45 seconds and the written policy decides
→ "take the call" is a warm transfer: the manager gets the brief and the last turns, and never asks for the order number

Most escalations need one bit from a human, not a whole conversation. Ask for the bit.
