"Two hundred and fifteen dollars. My plan is sixty-five. This is ridiculous."

Most voice agents answer that with "I understand your frustration, per our policy..." and lose the customer. The usual fix is a prompt line: be empathetic, transfer if needed. The model forgets how angry the caller was two turns ago, repeats the same empathy, and transfers on a whim.

What we built instead:
→ a frustration meter beside the LLM, scoring every turn from words and timing
→ talking over the agent, fast speech and repetition count, not just angry words
→ the score picks one move per reply: reflect back, name the feeling, offer a choice, take ownership
→ Cartesia sonic-3 slows down and softens as the call heats up
→ the handoff is a rule in code: 85, three hot turns, or two asks for a person

The part that makes finance teams comfortable: the $75 credit limit is enforced by the tool, not the prompt. A prompt is a request. A tool that refuses is a control.
