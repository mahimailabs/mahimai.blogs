"¿A qué hora es el desayuno?"

A good interpreter says "What time is breakfast?"
A typical voice assistant says "Breakfast is from seven to ten."

Fluent. Friendly. Wrong. The guest asked the desk, not the interpreter.

What it takes to build a live interpreter agent:

→ Speech to speech: one model hears any language and speaks any language. No language detection step in the middle.
→ Instructions that forbid helping, clause by clause: first person, questions stay questions, never summarize.
→ Captions paired on turns, not finals. A ten-second sentence is several "final" segments.
→ Two people, one session: relink to the active speaker, never while the agent is talking (echo).
